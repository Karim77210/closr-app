01 — The essence

A brand built around a single promise: closeness has value.

Closr isn't another social network. It's a private room where attention is earned and monetized. Everything, from the logo to the smallest word, should give one feeling: you're truly one-on-one.

01

Modern & tech

Clean, fast, frictionless. The interface steps back so the conversation can lead.

02

1:1 closeness

One to one, never the crowd. Every exchange is exclusive, direct, with no audience.

03

Warm & human

Warm tones, soft shapes. Technology in service of the bond, never the other way around.

02 — The name

Closr.

Short for “closer” — nearer. The dropped vowel, the way you clip a word with someone you know well. The name already says what the product does.

It's pronounced “closer,” always written with a capital C and the rest lowercase. Never “CLOSR” or “closR.”

03 — Logo explorations

Five directions.

Each route explores a different meaning of Closr: the bubble, the bond, the letter, the access, the word. Only one is chosen below — the others stay possible territories.

★ Chosen

ROUTE 01

The 1:1 Bubble

A message bubble holding two dots: two people, one conversation. Legible all the way down to a favicon.

ROUTE 02

The Bond

Two overlapping circles. The intersection — the intimacy — becomes the heart of the brand.

ROUTE 03

The Monogram

The “C” as an open ring. A door left ajar, ready to close around the exchange.

ROUTE 04

The Access

A stylized keyhole. The message as a privileged space you unlock.

clsr

ROUTE 05

The Logotype

The “o” replaced by a bubble. Type becomes the logo, with no separate symbol.

Why Route 01?

It says “messaging” at first glance, carries the 1:1 in its two dots, stays warm, and holds up at 16px as well as 96px.

09 — Tone of voice

Like a message to a trusted friend.

Closr is on first-name terms. Closr gets to the point, warmly. Never corporate, never too familiar, never sleazy.

Close

We talk to one person, never to “an audience.”

Direct

Short sentences, action verbs. We say what we do.

Reassuring

Privacy and respect at the core. We protect intimacy.

Warm

A touch of emotion, a well-placed emoji. Human first.

We say ✓

  • “Mia replied to you in private.”
  • “Here, it's one-on-one.”
  • “Unlock this message · $5”
  • “Write the first word.”

We avoid ✕

  • “A new message has been received.”
  • “Join our community!”
  • “Proceed with the transaction payment.”
  • “Hey babe 😏 come quick…”
